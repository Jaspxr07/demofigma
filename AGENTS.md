Commit Guidelines

Every commit must be a conventional commit.
Please follow the guidelines below to ensure that your commit messages are clear and consistent.

-Use present tense
-Use imperative mood
-Limit the subject line to 50 characters
-Capitalize the subject line
-Do not end the subject line with a period
-Use Feat: for new features
-Use Fix: for bug fixes


Security Guidelines

-Never commit secrets or sensitive information to the repository
